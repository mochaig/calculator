Before the calculator is considered complete it must be able to do ordered calculations correctly.
For example:
(3-4)/2		Result: -0.5
4*(6/3)		Result: 8
4-3+6/2		Result: -2
These examples are good for checks as they require the different operations established in the list of requirements as well as needing proper order of operatons to ge tthe intended result.
